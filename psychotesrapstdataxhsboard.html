This tab is tailored for psychotherapists and provides a detailed overview of therapy-related tasks, sessions, and patient progress.

Key Sections:

My Patients Table:

Displays a list of patients assigned to the psychotherapist.

Click on a patient’s name to open their individual treatment page, where therapy modules are structured according to the treatment protocol.

Treatment Progress Table:

Tracks each patient’s progress throughout the course of their therapy. This includes session outcomes, completed steps, and status updates.

Today’s Sessions Table:

Lists all sessions scheduled for the current day, helping the therapist manage their time and prepare accordingly.

Daily Tasks Table:

Shows tasks assigned for the day.

The "Contacts" button redirects to the patient’s profile page.

The "Details" button opens a section with review notes from yesterday’s sessions, allowing for reflection and continuity of care.

Clinical Team:

Lists key contacts relevant to patient support, including assigned doctors and the case manager, along with their contact information.
Clicking on a clinician's name opens their personal profile, which includes details, contact information, areas of expertise, and availability.
